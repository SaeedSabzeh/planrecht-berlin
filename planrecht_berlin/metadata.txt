about=Planrecht Berlin queries Berlin's public B-Plan WFS for adopted development plans at a clicked point. The experimental preview shows selected figures and provisions for the commercial and public road areas in Neukölln plan 8-2-1. Pankow 3-9 and Spandau 5-8 have plan-level summaries. Check the official plan and amendments for the full rules. The plugin sends in-city click coordinates to Berlin's public map service and uses no account or API key.
